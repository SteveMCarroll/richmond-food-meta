# Independent review — Richmond BC Chinese dining guide

Cold review performed per `research/REVIEW-BRIEF.md`. Reviewer read `research/METHODOLOGY.md`, `data/SCHEMA.md`, `PROJECT_PLAN.md`, all 27 files in `data/venues/`, all 6 files in `data/hubs/`, and `research/dim-sum-meta.md` / `research/empire-calibration.md` as historical notes. `site/content/restaurants.json` was not used as evidence. Live verification was performed against ~15 primary sources (restaurant official sites, news articles, an awards directory) and several of the platform sources the dataset relies on most heavily (RedNote, Fantuan). Findings and URLs are below.

Note on process: `research/METHODOLOGY.md`, `PROJECT_PLAN.md`, `research/dim-sum-meta.md`, and `research/empire-calibration.md` existed only as uncommitted edits in the main working tree at review time, not in this git worktree; their content was read from the main tree to ensure the current version was reviewed. `data/venues/`, `data/hubs/`, and `data/SCHEMA.md` were identical between the two trees.

## 1. Executive verdict

**Publishable with corrections.** The methodology is unusually self-aware for a project this size — it names its own biases (§12), separates status from confidence (§7), and asks reviewers to check for exactly the failure modes that actually recur in the dataset (§15). The dataset's operational facts (addresses, phones, hours) are, where independently checked, accurate: 10 of 10 official-site hour/phone/address checks matched exactly, including two that the dataset itself had already flagged as unstable (Golden Paramount's "COVID hours" notice, BBQ&Co's weekend-closure staffing update). That is a real achievement — the operational layer is trustworthy.

The quality layer is weaker than the confidence labels suggest. The dataset's two richest "detailed Chinese-language" evidence types — RedNote and Fantuan — are, as cited, **not independently verifiable by a reader or a future reviewer**: every RedNote URL tested resolved to the platform's generic app shell, not the cited post, and Fantuan pages returned encoded/opaque payloads rather than readable review content. This doesn't mean the underlying claims are false — the original researcher may have viewed them through an authenticated session — but it does mean roughly a third of the 27 venue records rest on evidence that cannot be reproduced or spot-checked from the files alone, which is a direct miss against the methodology's own reviewer checklist item 12 ("Could another agent reproduce or update the work from the files alone?"). Separately, 5 of 6 hub records make specific editorial and even quantitative claims with an empty `sources: []` array, which is a plain violation of Methodology §11 ("every source entry needs a descriptive label, direct URL, and observation date... strong claims in the body should be attributable to a listed source").

Neither issue is a fabrication — spot checks did not surface any invented address, phone, or hour, and one claim I expected to be unsupported (Lanxuan's chef-award signal) turned out to be true, just uncited. The issues are about **evidentiary transparency and hub-level rigor**, not honesty. They are fixable without a rewrite.

## 2. Findings by severity

### Critical (factual errors requiring correction)

None found. No address, phone number, or posted-hours claim that I could independently check turned out to be wrong. This is worth stating plainly given how much operational data the dataset carries.

### High (methodological risks that materially affect trust in the "quality" claims)

1. **RedNote citations are not verifiable from the published URLs.** Every `rednote.com/search_result/...` URL in the dataset (used in `data/venues/ban-bu-xian.md` x2, `hk-bbq-master.md`, `master-hung.md`, `parker-place-bbq.md`, `tak-fook.md`, `xiang-chuan-mansion.md`) resolves to RedNote's generic navigation shell, not the cited post, when fetched directly. This is very likely a platform access limitation (RedNote gates content behind login/app), not evidence the posts don't exist — but the methodology explicitly asks whether "another agent [could] reproduce or update the work from the files alone" (§15.12), and for this entire evidence class, the answer is no. The same single RedNote URL (`.../688a97000000000022038390`, labelled "RedNote 2025 Richmond siu-mei comparison") is cited as a load-bearing "detailed local/Chinese-language" signal for **four different venues** (HK BBQ Master, Master Hung, Parker Place BBQ, Tak Fook) — if it is in fact one comparison post naming several shops, that's a legitimate single source doing double duty, but the dataset doesn't state that explicitly, and a reviewer cannot confirm it either way.
2. **Fantuan citations are similarly opaque to verification.** `fantuanorder.com` pages returned base64/token-encoded content rather than readable reviews or scores when fetched. Fantuan evidence underpins `chengdu-street-food.md`, `geng-shi-ji.md`, `cloud-9.md`, `master-hung.md`, `parker-place-bbq.md`, `tak-fook.md`, `xiang-chuan-mansion.md`, `south-silk-road.md`, `jiang-jiang-xinjiang.md` — a majority of the dataset's `hot`/`rising` calls. The specific taste/service/environment sub-scores quoted in body text (e.g., Tak Fook "4.5 taste... 2.2 environment and service," Parker Place "4.2 taste versus 2.1 environment and 2.1 service") could not be confirmed or refuted directly; they read as plausible and internally consistent with the methodology's stated pattern, but they are asserted, not shown.
3. **Five of six hub records carry unsourced editorial claims.** `data/hubs/aberdeen.md`, `alexandra.md`, `capstan.md`, `parker.md`, and `yaohan.md` all have `sources: []`, yet each makes a specific status call and, in several cases, quantitative or trend claims: Aberdeen asserts a "5:00–6:30pm" dinner rush; Alexandra claims to be "where local sources point night diners" while citing none; Capstan is called "rising" and "repeatedly cited for dinner and late-night" with zero citations; Yaohan is flatly called "fading" ("has been declining for years") with zero citations. Only `empire.md` carries a source. This is the dataset's clearest violation of its own §11 rule ("strong claims in the body should be attributable to a listed source") and §2 commitment #10 ("keep every published factual claim traceable to a dated source").
4. **Capstan and Yaohan hubs are orphaned from the venue dataset.** No venue file references `hub: capstan` or `hub: yaohan` (confirmed by grepping all 27 `hub:` fields). Three venues (`geng-shi-ji.md`, `jade-seafood.md`, `lanxuan.md`) are physically in the Capstan area and correctly use `area: Capstan` / `hub: null` per the schema's stall-vs-standalone rule, but that leaves the `capstan` hub record describing a cluster the reader can't actually cross-reference to any specific business in the guide. The `yaohan` hub has no associated venues at all — its "fading" claim is unfalsifiable from within this dataset and unsourced from outside it.

### Medium (calibration and specificity gaps)

5. **`ban-bu-xian.md` status (`hot`) is a defensible but borderline call given the severity of its own cited evidence.** The record's July 2026 RedNote citation describes "30-plus-minute gaps between dishes and dismissive service after the move," which is close to a textbook description of the methodology's own `watch` definition ("material execution, operations, or evidence concerns remain," §7). `status` is correctly decoupled from quality by design, but the June/July evidence split (good food report vs. severe service failure, both post-move, only weeks apart) combined with unreconciled hours/phone arguably tips this toward `watch` rather than `hot` until the operational instability settles. Worth a deliberate re-read, not an automatic downgrade.
6. **`loon-fong-hotpot.md` is rated `confidence: high` on two sources, neither an independent quality signal.** Its `sources` list is the official site and a Google Maps centroid pin — no Chinese-language, transactional, or expert signal is cited at all, which is a literal miss against the admission rule's "at least one detailed local, Chinese-language, expert, or transactional signal" (§6), even though `status: watch` (not one of the three statuses the rule technically gates). `confidence: high` for a chain location that opened in Richmond BC roughly five months before `lastVerified` (confirmed via search: Loon Fong's Richmond BC unit opened February 25, 2026, the brand's first location outside Ontario) reads as generous. Separately worth flagging for future research: Loon Fong also operates in Richmond Hill, Ontario, and has a Chinese Restaurant Awards Elite 30 profile for that Ontario location — the shared "Richmond" name is a real cross-contamination risk for any future researcher pulling reviews or awards without checking city. The current record does not fall into this trap, but nothing in the file warns a future editor about it either.
7. **`lanxuan.md`'s strongest evidence is missing from its own sources list.** The body cites a "chef-recognition signal" as the reason to treat Lanxuan as a `rising` audit target, but none of the three cited sources (Zomi, Bravo, Wanderlog — all directory/hours pages) support that claim. Independent search confirms the claim is true and substantial: chef Lai Wei was named Chinese Restaurant Awards **Dim Sum Chef of the Year (2024)**, Lanxuan appears in the **2025 CRA Elite 30 Canada** list, and its BBQ pork bun won a 2024 Critics' Choice Signature Dish Award (https://chineserestaurantawards.com/list/2025/elite-30-canada/lanxuan-restaurant/, https://chineserestaurantawards.com/dim-sum-chef-of-the-year-2024/). This is a real, citable, high-value independent expert signal that the record alludes to but never actually attaches — an easy fix that would likely justify upgrading confidence from `medium` toward `high` and possibly the status designation.
8. **Regional concentration remains heavily Cantonese/HK despite the project's stated priority to counter that bias.** 19 of 27 venues (70%) are Cantonese/Hong Kong lineage (including HK café and siu-mei formats). This is explicitly acknowledged in `PROJECT_PLAN.md` and `METHODOLOGY.md` §12/14 as a known, actively-countered bias, and the current pass did add six non-Cantonese regional lanes (Sichuan, Hunan, Xinjiang, Dongbei, Yunnan, Shanghai/Jiangnan) — real progress — but each of those lanes is represented by exactly one venue, so a single closure, RedNote takedown, or bad night would erase the guide's entire coverage of that region. This is a coverage thinness issue more than a bias issue at this point.
9. **The "Muslim Northwest/Uyghur" gap listed in `PROJECT_PLAN.md` overlaps with existing coverage without the plan noting it.** `jiang-jiang-xinjiang.md` already covers Kazakh/Xinjiang dishes (hand-grabbed lamb rice, baked lamb buns, lamb soup). The remaining gap is more specific than the plan states — it's about Hui/Uyghur religious-identity dining (halal certification, Uyghur-run kitchens) rather than "Xinjiang cuisine" broadly, which is partially covered. Not an error, but the gap description could be sharper.
10. **Two Reddit threads could not be independently confirmed.** `research/dim-sum-meta.md`'s "May 2026 local discussion" (`reddit.com/.../1t1auxz/best_dim_sum_in_richmond/`) and the "June 2026 Richmond locals' BBQ-pork discussion" cited in `bbq-and-co.md` and `hk-bbq-master.md` (`.../1trg0tw/best_bbq_pork_on_rice/`) could not be fetched directly (Reddit blocks automated fetch in this environment) or located via search under those exact thread IDs. This is noted as an access limitation, not a claim of fabrication — but it means this reviewer, like a future researcher, cannot audit the single most-cited "local community discussion" source in the set.

### Optional (small precision improvements)

11. `south-silk-road.md` cites Restaurantji at a URL slug reading `cool-chinese-restaurant` — confirmed via fetch to correctly resolve to South Silk Road (likely a retained legacy listing name), not an error, but worth a cleaner citation label so a future reader isn't confused.
12. `south-silk-road.md`'s provisional midnight closing time is on the conservative side of its own sources — the cited Restaurantji page states Friday–Sunday hours run to 12:30am, not 12:00am. The record's caution to "call after 10:00pm" already covers this, so it's a rounding note, not a correction.
13. The Tourism Richmond Empire Centre article, used to classify Cha Don/One Cafe/Cloud 9, was last substantively updated June 27, 2025 — over a year before `lastVerified: 2026-07-19`. `research/empire-calibration.md` already flags this as needing "2026 re-verification," but that caution isn't carried into the individual venue records (`cha-don.md`, `one-cafe.md`, `cloud-9.md`) that actually cite the article as a source.

## 5. Recommended changes

### Must-fix (before calling this publishable without caveats)

- Add at least one citation to `data/hubs/aberdeen.md`, `alexandra.md`, `capstan.md`, `parker.md`, and `yaohan.md`, or soften the specific claims (the "5:00–6:30pm rush," "where local sources point," "repeatedly cited," "declining for years" language) to match what is actually sourced.
- Either link the Capstan-area standalone venues (`geng-shi-ji.md`, `jade-seafood.md`, `lanxuan.md`) into the `capstan` hub narrative explicitly in the hub's body text, or state plainly in the hub record that it currently describes a district with no directly-linked dataset venues.
- Reconsider `yaohan.md`'s unsourced "fading" claim — either source it or downgrade the certainty of the language until it is.
- Add the missing Chinese Restaurant Awards citation to `data/venues/lanxuan.md` (both the Dim Sum Chef of the Year 2024 page and the 2025 Elite 30 listing) — the evidence already exists and materially strengthens a currently thin record.
- Add a note to the RedNote-sourced records (`ban-bu-xian.md`, `hk-bbq-master.md`, `master-hung.md`, `parker-place-bbq.md`, `tak-fook.md`, `xiang-chuan-mansion.md`) and Fantuan-sourced records acknowledging that these platforms are not independently browsable from a bare URL — either by describing what was actually observed in more self-contained detail in the body text (so the claim doesn't depend on the reader reaching the source), or by noting the access method used (logged-in session, app, etc.).

### Later research (valuable but not blocking)

- Revisit `ban-bu-xian.md`'s status call once the post-move operational picture stabilizes (Medium #5).
- Add at least one independent signal to `loon-fong-hotpot.md` before keeping `confidence: high`; consider a Chinese-language or transactional source specific to the Richmond BC unit (not the Ontario locations).
- Deepen each single-venue regional lane (Xinjiang, Dongbei, Yunnan, Shanghai/Jiangnan) with a second candidate where possible, per `PROJECT_PLAN.md`'s own stated priorities.
- Pursue the still-open regional gaps: Chaoshan/Teochew, Hakka, Shandong/Beijing, and a sharper (halal/Uyghur-identity-specific) version of the Northwest-Chinese gap.
- Add dessert/bakery/boba format coverage — currently zero venues in a format that is a significant part of Richmond's actual Chinese food scene.
- Carry the Tourism Richmond Empire-guide staleness caution (currently only in `research/empire-calibration.md`) into `cha-don.md`, `one-cafe.md`, and `cloud-9.md` directly.
- Attempt to re-verify or replace the two Reddit citations that could not be confirmed in this pass.

## 6. What the original research got right

- **Operational accuracy is strong.** Every official-site hours/phone/address claim checked (Empire Seafood, Continental, Chef Tony, Fisherman's Terrace, Golden Paramount, BBQ&Co, Chengdu Street Food, Jade Seafood, Double Double, South Silk Road) matched the live source exactly, including messy edge cases the dataset had already flagged as unstable rather than smoothing them over.
- **The status/confidence separation is used correctly, not just described.** Records like `xiang-chuan-mansion.md` (hot + high) and `golden-paramount.md` (watch + medium, explicitly because of messy hours) show the distinction is doing real editorial work, not just filling a template.
- **Negative evidence is preserved rather than averaged away**, exactly as the methodology asks. `ban-bu-xian.md` keeps both a strong food report and a severe service-failure report side by side. `geng-shi-ji.md` and `tak-fook.md` explicitly retain low service/environment sub-scores next to strong taste signals instead of collapsing them into one number. This is the project's strongest methodological habit and it shows up consistently across the dataset, not just in the flagship examples.
- **Genuine new-platform leads are correctly gated, not oversold.** `meeting-in-changan.md` is marked `new-lead` / `lead` confidence and its body explicitly says "this is intentionally a lead, not a recommendation" — verified against the source Noms article, this is an accurate and appropriately cautious representation of thin, single-sourced, pre-opening evidence.
- **Stale-source vigilance is real, not performative.** Multiple records (Double Double, Sanbo, Lanxuan, South Silk Road) explicitly flag and reconcile conflicting third-party hour listings rather than picking one arbitrarily, and the two "COVID hours" / "staffing update" cases were both confirmed live and had already been correctly caught.
- **The project's own stated bias list (§12) is accurate self-diagnosis** — the Cantonese-weighting and single-venue-per-new-region issues this review surfaces are the same issues `PROJECT_PLAN.md` and `METHODOLOGY.md` already name as open work, which suggests the authors are tracking the right risks even where the current pass hasn't fully closed them.
