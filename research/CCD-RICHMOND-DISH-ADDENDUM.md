# CCD regional-dish addendum: Richmond, BC

_Working crosswalk for the Richmond food meta guide · researched 2026-07-30 · visit day Friday 2026-07-31_

This addendum maps all 99 dishes in `CCD_Regional_Dish_Catalog.md` to current Richmond, BC options. It deliberately separates an exact named-menu match from a restaurant that merely cooks the right regional cuisine.

## The short answer

If there is time for only two stops tomorrow:

1. **Lunch: Ajea Noodle, Yaohan Centre** — order the **Chengdu sweet-water noodles** first. Add a made-to-order claypot rice only if appetite allows; it takes roughly 15–20 minutes. This is the most unusual exact CCD match found in Richmond. Current Friday hours are reported as **11:00am–8:00pm**. 3700 No. 3 Road.
2. **Dinner: Xiang Chuan Mansion** — order **Chairman Mao's red-braised pork** and ask for the live Mandarin fish **steamed with chopped pepper**. Those are two exact Hunan matches; the fish and pork are sharing dishes, so this is best with 3–6 people. Current Friday hours are **11:30am–10:00pm**. 6091 No. 3 Road.

If the priority is the largest number of exact catalog dishes at one restaurant, go to **Szechuan Tales**: dan dan noodles, chili-oil wontons, kung pao chicken, twice-cooked pork, laziji, fish-fragrant pork, water-boiled beef, and mapo tofu are all on the current menu.

## Match key

- **Exact** — the current menu or a current specialist review names the dish.
- **Close** — same dish family or technique, but not the catalog's specific regional version.
- **Regional** — best Richmond specialist for that cuisine; the exact dish was not found.
- **Gap** — no credible current Richmond match found. This is useful negative data, not a recommendation failure.
- **Component** — a technique, sauce, or home-cooking format that is not a sensible standalone restaurant target.

“Exact” describes the dish match, not necessarily best-in-city quality. The recommended column combines menu evidence with the existing guide's specialist evidence where possible.

### Caveats to preserve in the public guide

- Restaurant menus, prices, seafood availability, and Friday hours can change. Call before a special trip for live fish, late-night dining, or a branch with incomplete hours.
- **Silkway Halal** has strong dish evidence but Friday hours were not independently verified; it should not be the plan-A stop tomorrow without a phone call.
- **Meeting in Chang'an** appears to serve roujiamo and lamb paomo, but its exact food-court unit and operating hours remain unverified. Old Xian is the safer visit-ready recommendation.
- A closed delivery listing for the former **YELIN Coconut Chicken Hotpot** is evidence against recommending it, not a current restaurant option.
- Several additions in this addendum (Ajea, Old Xian, Once More, Yin Ji, Tasty BBQ Beer Bar, Shanghai Dumpling King) are dish-specific leads that have not yet been promoted into the core venue dataset. Keep the stated confidence attached until they receive the guide's full two-signal venue review.
