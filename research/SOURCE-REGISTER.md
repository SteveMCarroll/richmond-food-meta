## Reproducible citation practice

For every platform observation, record the exact store/post/article URL, the observation date, and the visible fact—not just the platform name. For session-bound sources, the venue body must state what was observed and how it was accessed. A future agent should be able to understand the claim even if the source later requires login or disappears.

Do not count syndication as independence. A Sing Tao article quoting a restaurant's Instagram announcement is one underlying operational signal. A directory that summarizes Google reviews is not a new independent quality source.

## Verified platform-level findings

- OpenRice's current public region list omits Canada; the separate Canada travel archive is historical.
- Fantuan public web pages were readable without login on 2026-07-19, although only selected reviews and notes are exposed.
- Westca's Richmond directory and cuisine filters remain browsable, but dated detail reviews show low volume and uneven freshness.
- Sing Tao continues publishing Vancouver food stories in 2026; Ming Pao Canada ceased operations in January 2026.
- Richmond restaurant inspections are administered by Vancouver Coastal Health, not Fraser Health.

Recheck each platform quarterly or before a large refresh. Platform access and indexing change quickly; this file records a dated state, not a permanent guarantee.
