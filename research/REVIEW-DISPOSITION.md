# Independent review — disposition

**Disposition date: 2026-07-19.** This records what was accepted, modified, or deferred from `INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md` so another model does not repeat the same audit without seeing the editorial decisions.

## Accepted and applied

- Added dated sources or softened unsupported claims for all six hub records.
- Recast Yaohan from an unsourced decline verdict to `watch` and described the absence of linked venue audits.
- Connected the Capstan-area standalone venues to the hub narrative without falsely marking them as mall tenants.
- Added Chinese Restaurant Awards sources to Lanxuan.
- Reclassified Ban Bu Xian from `hot` to `watch` because its post-move execution evidence is materially unstable.
- Lowered Loon Fong Hotpot from high to medium confidence and documented the Richmond BC / Richmond Hill Ontario contamination risk.
- Carried source-age cautions into the Empire food-court venue records.
- Added self-contained access notes for RedNote, Fantuan, and Reddit observations.
- Sharpened the open coverage gap from generic Xinjiang to explicitly Hui/Uyghur/halal identity; the new Silkway record now fills the Hui/Ningxia halal lane, while Uyghur-run dining remains open.

## Adopted with correction

- **OpenRice:** added to the source model, but as archival Canadian evidence. The current OpenRice platform's region selector does not include Canada, and its indexed Vancouver archive is roughly 2009–2010—not a live 2026 Richmond review community.
- **Chinese-language media:** Sing Tao is current and useful in 2026. Ming Pao Canada is now an archive because its Canadian operation closed in January 2026.
- **Food safety:** Richmond is under Vancouver Coastal Health, not Fraser Health. Inspection evidence remains separate from taste and reputation.
- **Fantuan auditability:** the full app corpus is not public, but current public web pages do expose substantial dated evidence. Citations should state exactly which web-visible observations were used.
- **Public Chinese text:** Westca is added as a useful historical/local directory with explicit low-volume and staleness limits.

## New research added after review

- Szechuan Tales: a second serious Sichuan lane with current Fantuan demand and dish-level evidence.
- Silkway Halal Cuisine: Hui/Ningxia halal Chinese cooking, explicitly distinguished from Uyghur and generic Xinjiang labels.
- Top Shanghai Cuisine: a dedicated Shanghai/Jiangnan staple with a strong food-versus-service score split.
- Sing Yee: traditional Cantonese cooking and post-9:00pm `da laang`, filling a real late-night use case without calling it a dedicated Chaoshan restaurant.

## Deferred, not dismissed

- Dedicated Chaoshan/Teochew, Hakka, Shandong/Beijing, and Uyghur-run venues still need evidence-led sweeps.
- Dessert, bakery, boba, and breakfast outside Empire remain underrepresented.
- Current VCH record-level checks should be added when a safety claim is material; historical corrected incidents should not become permanent stigma.
- RedNote post permalinks should replace search-result URLs wherever the signed-in source allows it.
