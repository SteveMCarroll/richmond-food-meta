# Richmond dim sum meta — 2026 working note

This is a candidate audit, not a final ranking. Updated July 19, 2026.

## Family fit

- No dietary restrictions.
- Jum prefers fewer crowds, but a crowd is acceptable when the experience merits it.
- Flexible timing makes first seating, early-bird, and late lunch viable.
- Empire Seafood is a known family benchmark.
- The previously visited restaurant in the "big mall" may be Fisherman's Terrace in Aberdeen Centre; confirm later.
- Saturday dim sum is a poor fit because the 70mm *Odyssey* screening is in Langley at 11:00 a.m. Sunday is the natural dim sum day.

## What appears current

A May 2026 Richmond community discussion repeatedly recommends four established choices:

1. **Continental Seafood Restaurant** for classic Cantonese and Richmond's surviving push-cart experience.
2. **Empire Seafood Restaurant** for a comfortable room, online reservations, early opening, and dependable family dim sum.
3. **Fisherman's Terrace Seafood Restaurant / 釣魚台海鮮酒家** for polished traditional Cantonese dim sum in Aberdeen Centre.
4. **Chef Tony Seafood Restaurant** for higher-end, more modern dim sum, with noise/crowding as a recurring caution.

Other current candidates mentioned by local diners or recent award coverage include Golden Paramount, Jade Seafood, Lanxuan, Yue, Fortune Terrace, Sea Fortune, Kirin, Tin Tin, and Neptune. Each needs identity, status, and 2026 quality verification before inclusion.

## Preliminary archetypes

### Empire Seafood Restaurant / 帝苑皇宴海鮮酒家

- Known family benchmark; current local discussion still rates it highly.
- Officially lists dim sum daily from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
- Online reservations and an earlier opening make it the easiest known option for crowd avoidance.
- A May 2026 local comment specifically praises the less-cramped environment and SkyTrain view.
- Research question: Is the cooking still strong enough to repeat, or merely the best logistical fit?

### Continental Seafood Restaurant

- Current official hours list lunch/dim sum from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
- Multiple April–June 2026 local discussions identify it as Richmond's only remaining push-cart dim sum room.
- Likely the most distinctive experience, but weekend reports warn about a packed entrance and difficult parking.
- Research question: Can a 10:00 a.m. reservation deliver the carts without the peak crowd, and are cart items circulating fresh at opening?

### Fisherman's Terrace / 釣魚台海鮮酒家

- Aberdeen Centre, third floor; dim sum officially served 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. daily.
- Long-running polished Cantonese benchmark with repeated 2026 recommendations.
- Likely the family's unnamed "big mall" restaurant.
- Research question: Does current execution justify revisiting it over a new experience, and how noisy is first seating?

### Chef Tony Seafood Restaurant

- Empire Centre; official dim sum hours are 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.
- Modern, higher-end Cantonese dim sum with longstanding awards and specialty items.
- Current local discussion calls it good but notably noisy.
- Research question: Which dishes show real technique and which are luxury garnish, and can a reservation reduce the chaotic-room problem?

### Golden Paramount Seafood Restaurant / 金百樂海鮮酒家

- Smaller technique-focused Cantonese candidate with a concise menu and a strong historical reputation among Chinese Restaurant Awards judges.
- Its website presents conflicting current hours (9:00 or 10:30 opening), so phone/current-platform verification is required.
- Research question: Does the smaller room offer better cooking and a calmer meal, or does it create a worse wait?

### Jade Seafood Restaurant / 玉庭軒

- Officially open daily 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. and 5:00–9:00 p.m.
- Tourism Richmond currently advertises 20% off before 11:00 a.m., which could align value, freshness, and lower crowds.
- Research question: Is the early seating fully operational and are its contemporary/fusion items genuinely good?

### Lanxuan and Yue

- Both appear in recent local recommendations; Lanxuan's chef received a recent Dim Sum Chef of the Year honor, while Yue is repeatedly named by experienced local diners.
- Current official information and Chinese-language dish-level evidence remain incomplete.
- Research question: Are these the updated-meta choices that should displace one of the familiar legacy rooms?

## Tentative trip strategy

- **Best logistical default:** Sunday at Empire Seafood's 9:00 a.m. opening.
- **Most distinctive old-school experience:** Continental at 10:00 a.m. with a reservation, accepting some bustle for the carts.
- **Potential new discovery:** first seating at Jade, Golden Paramount, Lanxuan, or Yue if Chinese-source research establishes a clear cooking advantage.
- **Known polished option:** Fisherman's Terrace first seating, especially if the family wants a proven repeat.
- **Modern special-occasion option:** Chef Tony, only if noise and crowd evidence can be managed.

No selection should be made until recent Chinese-language reviews, reservation availability, and current signature dishes are checked.
