How we research

This is a dated, curated guide—not a universal ranking. We follow dish-level evidence, repeat local behaviour, and current operations, while preserving disagreement and uncertainty.

Ratings are not a common currency

We do not add a numerical “Chinese reviewers grade harder” correction. A food-court stall, banquet room, delivery brand, and polished chain have different review cultures. We compare like with like and read why diners praise or criticize a place: food, technique, service, value, queue, parking, atmosphere, or hygiene.

What counts as evidence

  • Current operations: official restaurant and mall pages, ordering systems, licences, and health-inspection records.
  • Chinese transaction signals: Fantuan sales, repeat orders, deals, and taste-versus-service patterns.
  • Detailed local reports: RedNote and other Chinese-language accounts that name dishes, dates, repeat visits, and changes.
  • Public community sources: local forums and, when current Canadian coverage can be verified, OpenRice and Chinese-language media.
  • Expert and editorial sources: Chinese Restaurant Awards and dated local reporting.
  • Maps and directories: useful for identity, hours, photos, and timing—not as sole proof of quality.

Signed-in RedNote and Fantuan pages may not reopen from a bare link. When they are used, the venue record summarizes exactly what was observed and seeks public operational corroboration. Publicly indexed OpenRice Canada pages found in this pass appeared archival rather than a live 2026 review corpus, so they are not presented as current consensus.

Status and confidence answer different questions

Hot
Strong current demand and evidence.
Rising
A contender gaining durable local use.
Staple
Established, current, and still useful.
Watch
Worth tracking, with material uncertainty or execution risk.
Lead
A candidate that still needs basic verification.
Confidence
How strongly the available evidence supports the record—not how fashionable the venue is.

Freshness and limitations

Every record carries a last-verified date and dated source links. Unknown phones, hours, coordinates, or reservation methods remain blank rather than guessed. Before a special visit, recheck hours and reservations directly—especially for new, moved, late-night, or food-court venues.

The current collection is strongest on Cantonese/Hong Kong dining. Its Sichuan, Hunan, Xinjiang, Dongbei, Yunnan, and Jiangnan/Shanghai coverage is deliberately growing but still shallower. The guide is a research snapshot, not a census of every Chinese restaurant in Richmond.

Safety evidence

Richmond restaurant inspections are handled by Vancouver Coastal Health. Inspection findings are kept separate from food quality and authenticity: an infraction is read in context, a reopening is recorded, and “no infractions” is never treated as a culinary endorsement.
