## Evidence and current read

Sing Yee fills a daypart and technique gap more than a regional-counting gap. The official Chinese site says the post-9:00pm menu is traditional 打冷, with a larger Cantonese repertoire that includes squab, winter-melon soup, eight-treasure duck, preserved-vegetable dishes, roast meats, and advance-order banquet cooking. Recent 2025–2026 reports continue to describe late groups, strong clay-pot and roast dishes, and attentive service.

The regional label is deliberately narrow: Hong Kong `da laang` is shaped by Teochew late-night culture, but Sing Yee self-identifies as traditional Cantonese. The guide should not count it as dedicated Chaoshan coverage. It is a `staple` with medium confidence because operations and current use are strong, while the best dish-level specialist profile is older.
