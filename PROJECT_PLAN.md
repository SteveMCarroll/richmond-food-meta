# Richmond BC Chinese dining guide — project plan

Last aligned with the dataset and methodology: 2026-07-30.

## Product

Build a small, fast, static guide to the current Chinese dining landscape in Richmond, British Columbia. The site is organized by hub, region, format, daypart, and evidence status rather than a single popularity ranking.

The public product is a reusable dining guide. Family-specific itineraries, names, lodging, tickets, and private constraints stay in `research/` or another private artifact and are not compiled into the public venue dataset.

## Source of truth

- `data/SCHEMA.md`: publishing contract.
- `data/site.md`: public site metadata.
- `data/hubs/*.md`: hub/cluster records.
- `data/venues/*.md`: venue records and evidence notes.
- `research/METHODOLOGY.md`: canonical research method.
- `research/REVIEW-BRIEF.md`: instructions for a cold independent review.
- `site/content/restaurants.json`: generated output; never hand-edit.

- All inherited records received a first operational enrichment pass.
- Current expansion covers dish-specific Hong Kong barbecue plus multiple Sichuan and Shanghai/Jiangnan options, Hunan, Xinjiang, Dongbei, Yunnan, Hui/Ningxia halal, and late-night Cantonese `da laang` lanes.
- Every venue has a dated source list and an evidence body.
- Operational blanks and source disagreements are preserved rather than guessed.
- A dedicated Chinese Cooking Demystified addendum preserves all 99 catalog
  dishes as exact, close, regional, gap, or component records. It joins to core
  venues by ID and keeps unpromoted dish leads distinct from venue recommendations.

## Research priorities

1. Independent methodological and content review from a separate model/task.
2. Resolve high-value operational blanks: standalone coordinates, stall phones, incomplete weekly schedules, and reservation methods.
3. Deepen the dim-sum lane from operational comparison to dish/technique evidence.
4. Audit regional gaps: Chaoshan, Hakka, Shandong/Beijing, explicitly halal-certified or Hui/Uyghur-identity dining beyond the existing Xinjiang stall, desserts, bakeries, late-night noodles, and any important format the current set misses.
5. Recheck moves and unstable records, especially `hot`, `rising`, `watch`, `lead`, and `new-lead` entries.
6. Improve hub records with dated sources and daypart evidence.
7. Maintain the dated public-Chinese-source stack: Fantuan web detail pages and Sing Tao for current evidence; Westca and OpenRice Canada for explicitly historical context; Ming Pao Canada as a closed archive.
8. Add Vancouver Coastal Health inspection checks when a safety, hygiene, closure, or reopening claim is material.

## Editorial principles

- Use the source roles and admission rules in `research/METHODOLOGY.md`.
- Prefer dish-specific and use-case-specific conclusions to universal restaurant rankings.
- Compare ratings only within meaningful platform/format contexts; do not apply an ethnicity-based numerical correction.
- Treat Chinese-language evidence as essential but not inherently unbiased.
- Separate food evidence from service, environment, queue, parking, and hygiene evidence.
- Preserve disagreement, stale-source warnings, and blank fields.
- Keep public guide records free of private itinerary details.

## Quality gates

Before treating a venue as a strong recommendation:

- identity and current location are resolved;
- operations are current enough for the venue's stability;
- at least two genuinely independent signals support the read;
- at least one detailed local, Chinese-language, expert, or transactional signal is present;
- the record names a defensible dish, technique, or use-case;
- status and confidence match the evidence.

Before publishing a data refresh:

- all YAML parses;
- every venue `id` equals its filename;
- all referenced hubs exist;
- every source has a direct URL and observation date;
- strong body claims map to listed sources;
- compiled output is regenerated by the frontend pipeline, not manually edited;
- a reviewer checks for overclaiming, source dependence, regional bias, and operational staleness.

## Update model

Research agents edit only `data/` and `research/` unless explicitly assigned frontend work. Frontend agents own compilation and presentation. `lastVerified` changes only after an actual record-level check, never as a bulk freshness gesture.
