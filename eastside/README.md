# Seattle Eastside — Chinese food research knowledgebase

This is an independent knowledgebase for Chinese and Taiwanese dining on the
Seattle Eastside. It does not read from, write to, or compile the Richmond BC
guide. The shared concepts are editorial only: evidence is dated, operational
facts are separated from food judgments, and venues are organized by cluster,
regional lane, dish, and daypart rather than one universal ranking.

## Scope

The working geography is Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Issaquah, Sammamish, and
Factoria, with Bel-Red and Overlake treated as cross-city dining corridors.
Seattle proper, Renton, Bothell, and the rest of King County are comparison
markets, not part of the core dataset.

`data/` is the publishing source of truth. `research/` records the method,
sources, unresolved conflicts, and next-pass leads. No generated website or
compiled JSON is included; another agent can build that from this corpus.

## Current pass

The initial research pass was completed on 2026-07-20. It establishes the
geography, high-value anchors, operational baselines, and explicit uncertainty.
It is a curated launching point, not a census and not yet a substitute for
firsthand dining across every regional lane.
