# Eastside coverage audit

Audit date: 2026-07-20

## What the corpus supports

- **Bellevue:** the center of gravity, split between specialist-rich Bel-Red and
  polished, group-friendly downtown.
- **Redmond:** a credible second tier with 151 Days, Hakka House, and Dong Ting
  Chun; several new or unresolved branches remain in the lead ledger.
- **Factoria:** a rising prepared-food and grocery-driven hub anchored by T&T,
  not merely a mall footnote.
- **Kirkland and Issaquah:** small, useful specialist pockets. The evidence does
  not support describing either as a deep district.
- **Sammamish:** deliberately thin; August Moon represents neighborhood use, not
  a destination scene.

## Corpus counts

- 8 hub records
- 27 venue records
- 7 municipalities/areas explicitly represented: Bellevue, Factoria, Redmond,
  Kirkland, Issaquah, Sammamish, and Overlake/Bel-Red subareas
- 14 additional candidates or openings in the change ledger

## Gaps for the next pass

1. In-person or branch-specific verification in Kirkland and Sammamish.
2. A fresh Cantonese seafood/dim-sum comparison for Factoria and Redmond.
3. Better first-party identity resolution for Regent Bakery and Facing East.
4. A post-opening read on Supreme Dumplings Redmond, BA Bakehouse, and Mama Xita.
5. Dietary metadata (vegetarian range, allergen handling, halal certification)
   only after direct verification; it has not been inferred.

## Bias controls

The corpus separates popularity, operational certainty, and editorial confidence.
It does not equate Asian population share with restaurant quality, and it does not
force every city to have the same number of recommendations.
