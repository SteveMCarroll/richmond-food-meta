## Operations and safety

First-party location, ordering, and mall pages are preferred for address and
hours. They establish operations, not quality. The public [King County restaurant
safety search](https://cd.kingcounty.gov/en/dept/dph/health-safety/food-safety/search-restaurant-safety-ratings)
is the authoritative route for inspection status; inspection grades are not used
as food-quality scores.
