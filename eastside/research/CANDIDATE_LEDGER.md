## Watch-list rules

- A newly opened branch does not inherit the flagship's confidence.
- “Opening soon” never becomes a venue record until current operation is verified.
- Directory-only evidence may support a lead, not a high-confidence recommendation.
- Chains are included only when a branch fills a real daypart, group, or geographic
  need; coverage is not a completeness contest.
